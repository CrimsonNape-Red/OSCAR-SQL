# Statistics

The **Statistics** page is a summary report of your therapy. It is the page OSCAR usually shows after an import. Open it with the **Statistics** button on the left, *View → View Statistics*, or <kbd>F4</kbd>.

[[idx:Statistics]]

## Report modes

[[idx:Report mode]]
At the top of the page, choose the **Report Mode**:

Standard
:   Columns for the most recent day, the last week, last 30 days, last 6 months and last year.

Monthly
:   One column per month.

Date Range
:   A range you choose.

**Report Date** sets the last day the report covers, so you can look back at an earlier period.

## What the report contains {.noindex}

[[idx:Reports!Statistics page]]
CPAP Usage
:   Days used, compliance (days meeting your [compliance threshold](preferences.md#clinical-tab)), and average hours per night.

Therapy Efficacy
:   AHI (or RDI) and the index for each type of event.

Leak Statistics
:   Average, upper percentile and other leak figures.

Pressure Statistics
:   Pressure figures for your therapy mode.

Oximetry
:   SpO2 and pulse summaries, when you have oximetry data.

Device information
:   [[idx:Device settings changes]] A table of each device, when it was first and last used, and every change of settings, with the dates each setting was in use. *Combine similar machines* and *Include Serial Number* in [Preferences → Appearance](preferences.md#other-visual-settings) control this table.

How averages and maximums are calculated follows the [Preferred Calculation Methods](preferences.md#preferred-calculation-methods). *Alternating Color Spacing* in [Preferences → Appearance](preferences.md#statistics) shades every second or third row to make the table easier to read.

## Personal data

When *View → Show Personal Data* is ticked, the report shows the name and details from your [profile](profiles.md).

## Printing

[[idx:Printing!Statistics]]
*File → Print Report* (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>P</kbd>) prints the page you are viewing. Turn on *Print reports in black and white* in [Preferences → Appearance](preferences.md#other-visual-settings) for printers without colour.
