# Glossary

Terms you will meet in OSCAR. *Help → Sleep Disorder Terms Glossary* opens a longer glossary online.

## Therapy and machines {.noindex}

[[idx:CPAP]] [[idx:APAP]] [[idx:Bilevel]] [[idx:ASV]] [[idx:EPR]] [[idx:Ramp]]
CPAP
:   Continuous Positive Airway Pressure. A machine that blows air at a steady pressure to keep the airway open during sleep.

APAP (AutoSet, Auto CPAP)
:   A CPAP machine that adjusts its pressure through the night, within limits, in response to your breathing.

Bilevel (BiPAP, VPAP, BPAP)
:   A machine with two pressures: a higher one when breathing in (IPAP) and a lower one when breathing out (EPAP).

IPAP / EPAP
:   Inspiratory and expiratory positive airway pressure: the pressures for breathing in and breathing out.

ASV
:   Adaptive Servo-Ventilation. A bilevel machine that varies its pressure support breath by breath, mainly to treat central and complex sleep apnea.

AVAPS / iVAPS
:   Bilevel modes that adjust pressure to deliver a target breathing volume or ventilation.

Backup rate (BU, ST)
:   A minimum breathing rate: if you do not take a breath in time, the machine triggers one.

EPR, C-Flex, A-Flex, P-Flex, Bi-Flex
:   Pressure relief: the machine lowers its pressure briefly when you breathe out, to make exhaling more comfortable. EPR is ResMed's name; the Flex names are Philips Respironics'.

Ramp
:   A period at the start of the night when pressure starts low and rises gradually.

Session
:   One continuous period of use, from mask on to mask off.

Summary-only data
:   Usage, settings and nightly totals, without detailed graphs.

cmH2O
:   Centimetres of water: the unit for CPAP pressure.

## Events and indices {.noindex}

[[idx:AHI]] [[idx:RDI]] [[idx:Apnea]] [[idx:Hypopnea]] [[idx:RERA]] [[idx:Flow limitation]] [[idx:Clear airway]] [[idx:Large leak]] [[idx:Periodic breathing]]
AHI
:   Apnea–Hypopnea Index: the number of apneas and hypopneas per hour of use. Under 5 is usually considered normal.

RDI
:   Respiratory Disturbance Index: like AHI, but also counting RERAs.

Apnea
:   A pause in breathing, or a large drop in airflow, lasting at least 10 seconds.

Obstructive apnea (OA)
:   An apnea where the airway is blocked although you are trying to breathe.

Clear airway (CA) / Central apnea
:   An apnea where the airway is open but you are not trying to breathe. ResMed calls these *Central Apneas* or *Clear Airway*; Philips calls them *Clear Airway*.

Unclassified apnea (UA) / Apnea (A)
:   An apnea the machine could not classify as obstructive or central.

Hypopnea (H)
:   A partial reduction in airflow lasting at least 10 seconds.

RERA
:   Respiratory Effort-Related Arousal: a series of breaths with increasing effort that ends in a brief awakening.

Flow limitation (FL)
:   A flattening of the breath's flow curve, showing that the airway is partly narrowed.

Vibratory snore (VS)
:   Snoring detected by the machine.

Periodic breathing (PB) / Cheyne-Stokes respiration (CSR)
:   Breathing that waxes and wanes in a regular cycle, sometimes with central apneas.

Large leak (LL)
:   Leak too high for the machine to measure your breathing reliably.

Unintentional leak
:   Air escaping from places other than the mask's vent: around the mask seal or through the mouth.

Total leak
:   All air leaving the system, including the mask's intended vent flow.

User flags
:   Events flagged by OSCAR itself rather than by the machine (see [Custom event flagging](preferences.md#custom-cpap-user-event-flagging)).

## Breathing measurements {.noindex}

[[idx:Flow rate]] [[idx:Tidal volume]] [[idx:Minute ventilation]] [[idx:Respiratory rate]]
Flow rate
:   The airflow in and out, breath by breath, in litres per minute.

Tidal volume
:   The volume of air in each breath.

Minute ventilation
:   The volume of air breathed per minute.

Respiratory rate
:   Breaths per minute.

## Oximetry {.noindex}

[[idx:SpO2]] [[idx:Desaturation]] [[idx:Pulse rate]]
SpO2
:   Peripheral oxygen saturation: the percentage of your blood's haemoglobin carrying oxygen, measured by a pulse oximeter. Usually 95–100% when awake.

Desaturation
:   A drop in SpO2 from the baseline, often following an apnea.

Baseline
:   The SpO2 level desaturations are measured from (see [Preferences → Oximetry](preferences.md#spo2-drop-baseline)).

Pulse rate
:   Heartbeats per minute.

## Statistics {.noindex}

[[idx:Percentile]] [[idx:Median]] [[idx:Compliance]]
Median
:   The middle value: half the readings are below it and half above.

Percentile
:   The value below which a given share of readings fall. The 95th percentile is exceeded only 5% of the time.

Compliance
:   Using therapy for at least a set number of hours a night, often 4 hours on 70% of nights.

## OSCAR terms {.noindex}

Profile
:   The data for one person. See [Profiles](profiles.md).

Device
:   A CPAP machine, oximeter or other recorder whose data is in a profile.

Day split time
:   The time of day at which OSCAR starts a new day. See [Preferences](preferences.md#session-splitting-settings).

Clinical Mode / Permissive Mode
:   Whether sessions can be disabled. See [Preferences → Clinical](preferences.md#clinical-tab).

`.oscar` file
:   A profile backup or share package. See [Backing up a profile](export-backup-share.md#backing-up-a-profile).
